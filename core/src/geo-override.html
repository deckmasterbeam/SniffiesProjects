Location Spoofing

Turn this on, then use Sniffies' own Travel Mode: tap the plane icon on the map, drop the pin where you want, then tap "Travel here". This extension picks that up automatically and keeps spoofing it — including for the app's own GPS checks — until you turn it off.

Note: this can pick up a location you search for by city name, but it can't pick up a location you reach only by dragging the map afterward without confirming.
