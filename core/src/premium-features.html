Premium Features

You have Sniffies Plus, so you don't need these features.

Unlocks things Sniffies normally reserves for Plus: location spoofing, opening profiles outside your boundary, and hiding photos you've sent.

Use the built-in Sniffies "Travel Mode" to select a location to travel to. From that mode, search for anywhere and click "Travel here". This can pick up a location you search for by name, but not one you reach by manually dragging the map.

Profiles outside your boundary open directly. Recommend opening in a new tab, current tab triggers current page reload.

Adds Sniffies' Hide Photo action to photos you've sent, so you can hide them again without Plus. Nothing to configure.
